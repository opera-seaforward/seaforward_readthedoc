Presented against the OceanPrediction DCC architecture, SEA-FORWARD is legible to
the wider community: every piece has a named role in a shared, published reference.
The upstream data, the single reproducible environment, the interoperable
CF-NetCDF output, the downstream tools, the versioned repository, and the restart
loop are not SEA-FORWARD idiosyncrasies — they are the standard components of an
operational ocean-prediction service, assembled here into a working, teachable,
reproducible whole. That is exactly the outcome the OPERA capacity-development
programme aims for, and the reason SEA-FORWARD is built the way it is.
