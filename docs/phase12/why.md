Mapping SEA-FORWARD onto the OceanPrediction DCC architecture gives every piece a name
from a shared, published reference. The upstream data, the single reproducible
environment, the NetCDF output, the downstream tools, the versioned repository and the
restart loop are not SEA-FORWARD idiosyncrasies — they are the standard components of an
operational ocean-prediction service, assembled here into a working whole that another
institution could rebuild from this documentation.

That is what the OPERA capacity-development programme aims for, and the reason
SEA-FORWARD is built the way it is.
