Rivers pour **freshwater** into the ocean. Near the coast this lowers the salinity,
creates buoyant **river plumes**, and changes how the water is layered. If your model
ignores rivers, its coastal salinity will be wrong.

CROCO adds rivers as **point sources**: at chosen grid cells, it injects a volume of
water per second (m³/s), with a chosen temperature and salinity. Our job is to:

1. get river discharge data for our region,
2. turn it into a file CROCO understands (`croco_runoff.nc`),
3. tell CROCO to use it (edit two config files, recompile),
4. make it run automatically every forecast (the `--rivers` flag).

We use a **climatology** — the *typical* seasonal discharge, repeating every year — so
the forecast always has valid river data for any date without downloading anything live.
