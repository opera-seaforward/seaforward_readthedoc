We use a ready-made, published dataset already on disk:

```text
~/seaforward/data/DATASETS_CROCOTOOLS/RUNOFF_DAI/Dai_Trenberth_runoff_global_clim.nc
```

It holds the monthly-mean discharge of the **925 largest rivers on Earth**, each with
its mouth longitude and latitude and its name — the Senegal and the Gambia on this
coast, and everything from the Amazon down to rivers of a few hundred m³/s.

To look inside the file and confirm it is there:

```bash
conda activate seaforward
ncdump -h ~/seaforward/data/DATASETS_CROCOTOOLS/RUNOFF_DAI/Dai_Trenberth_runoff_global_clim.nc | head -30
```

`ncdump -h` prints the header — the list of variables — of a NetCDF file. You should
see `FLOW_clm`, the monthly climatology in m³/s, and `lon_mou` and `lat_mou`, the river
mouth positions.
