Tides are the physics no global product carries. For anything on a shelf — sea
level extremes, tidal currents, tide-driven mixing, sediment transport — the tide
is often the dominant signal, and a tide-free model simply does not have it. The
tidal-range map (2–3.6 m on the Agulhas Bank, near-zero offshore) is the clearest
statement of what was added: real coastal physics that Mercator, at any
resolution, structurally cannot reproduce.

The limit is the same as everywhere else in this documentation: we can prove the
tide is **present and physically shaped** (the M2 oscillation, the shelf
amplification, the clean daily-mean match), but proving it is *accurate* needs
independent data — coastal tide gauges, along-track altimetry — not the reanalysis
that seeded the run. That is a validation chapter, not a setup one.
